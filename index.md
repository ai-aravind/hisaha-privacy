# HiSaha — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

HiSaha is an offline reminder and water-tracking app for Android (package `com.hisaha.app`), developed by Aravind Raghunathan and co-developed with Sahana. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

**The app does not collect, send or share any of your data.** Everything stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores these things on your device only:

- the reminders and categories you create, and when you completed them
- your water log, water goal and cup sizes
- your first name (if you enter one), used only for greetings
- your app settings, including the greeting Saha speaks

None of this leaves your phone through the app. HiSaha does not request internet access, has no accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking tools.

## Backups

- **Your own backup files.** In Settings you can save a backup file wherever you choose and restore it later. That file is under your control.
- **Android device backup.** If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why the app uses it |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show your reminders. |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | To deliver reminders at the time you chose. |
| Run at startup | To set your reminders again after the phone restarts. |
| Vibrate | For reminder vibration and tap feedback. |
| Display over other apps (optional) | To show Saha's animated card over other apps when a reminder arrives. You can leave this off. |

## Voice

Saha speaks using the text-to-speech engine already on your phone, restricted to voices that work offline. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

## Links

The Settings screen contains two links: one to the developer's LinkedIn profile and one to this privacy policy. Each opens in your browser only if you tap it.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
