# HiSaha — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

HiSaha is an offline reminder and water-tracking app for Android and iPhone (app ID `com.hisaha.app` on both), developed by Aravind Raghunathan and co-developed with Sahana. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to both versions; where they differ, it says so.

## The short version

**The app does not collect, send or share any of your data.** Everything stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores these things on your device only:

- the reminders and categories you create, and when you completed them
- your water log, water goal and cup sizes
- your first name (if you enter one), used only for greetings
- your app settings, including the greeting Saha speaks

None of this leaves your phone through the app. HiSaha makes no network connections, has no accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking tools. On Android it does not even request internet access.

On iPhone, the app's widgets and its Control Centre control read and update the same data through a storage area shared only between HiSaha and its own widgets, on your device.

## Backups

- **Your own backup files.** In Settings you can save a backup file wherever you choose and restore it later. That file is under your control.
- **Android device backup.** If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.
- **iPhone backup.** If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the backup may include the app's data. That is handled by Apple under your Apple Account settings, not by us.

## Permissions

### Android

| Permission | Why the app uses it |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show your reminders. |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | To deliver reminders at the time you chose. |
| Run at startup | To set your reminders again after the phone restarts. |
| Vibrate | For reminder vibration and tap feedback. |
| Display over other apps (optional) | To show Saha's animated card over other apps when a reminder arrives. You can leave this off. |

### iPhone

| Permission | Why the app uses it |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show your reminders and sip reminders, with Done, Snooze and drink buttons. |
| Time Sensitive notifications | So a reminder you mark as high priority can still appear while a Focus is on. You can turn this off in your iPhone's Settings. |

## Voice

Saha speaks using the text-to-speech voices already on your phone (Android's text-to-speech engine, restricted to voices that work offline, or the voices built into iOS). The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

## Links

The Settings screen contains two links: one to the developer's LinkedIn profile and one to this privacy policy. Each opens in your browser only if you tap it.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
